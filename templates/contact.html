Frequently asked

Common questions answered.

What is your return policy?

We offer free returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unworn and in original packaging. Contact our team for a prepaid return label.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically arrives within 3-5 business days. Express and Priority options are available at checkout for faster delivery.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries. Shipping costs and times vary by location. Import duties may apply depending on your country.

Can I modify my order?

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us within 2 hours of purchase and we'll be happy to help make changes.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes! Digital and physical gift cards are available in any amount from GH₵ 50 to GH₵ 500. Perfect for the minimalist in your life.

How do I care for my items?

Each piece comes with detailed care instructions. Generally, machine wash cold and lay flat to dry to preserve quality and longevity.
